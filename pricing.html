FAQ

Common Questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Is there a setup fee?

No. All plans include free onboarding and setup. We help you get started at no additional cost.

Do you offer discounts for annual billing?

Yes. Annual billing includes a 15% discount compared to monthly billing. Contact our team for details.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, UPI and net banking. Enterprise invoicing is available for Premium plans.
