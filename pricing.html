FAQ

Common Questions

Are the subscription prices monthly or per employee?

IN4 Attend and IN4 People are fixed monthly subscription plans, covering up to 50 and 100 employees respectively. Enterprise pricing is customised.

Can I purchase additional modules separately?

Yes. You can extend your subscription with optional modules such as Geo-tagging, Visitor Management, Parking, Canteen Management, Recruitment, Performance Management and AI Video Analytics.

Is Geo-tagging included in the subscription?

No. Geo-tagging is an optional paid add-on, available with compatible plans.

Can I upgrade from IN4 Attend to IN4 People?

Yes. You can upgrade your subscription as your business requirements grow.

What if my employee count exceeds the package limit?

Contact our team for an additional employee slab or an upgrade quotation.

Are biometric devices included in the subscription?

The software supports biometric device integration. Hardware purchase, installation and related charges are quoted separately where applicable.

Do you offer cloud and on-premise deployment?

Yes. IN4 CloudHR supports cloud and on-premise deployment options, subject to the selected package and commercial arrangement.

Are implementation and customisation included?

Standard onboarding and implementation scope will be confirmed during the proposal process. Customisation, special integrations and additional deployment requirements may attract separate charges.

Are taxes included in the displayed prices?

Prices are exclusive of applicable GST.
