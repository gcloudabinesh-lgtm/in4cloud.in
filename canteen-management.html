FAQ

Questions canteen teams ask us

From ₹2,499 per location / month Indicative, exclusive of GST. All pricing

Do employees need an app?

No. They identify at the kiosk with their face, fingerprint or card. Digital ordering from a phone is available as well.

Can guests eat at the canteen too?

Yes. Guest meals have their own rates, and the employer's share is recorded separately from employee meals.

How are subsidies handled?

Set the meal price and the employee and employer contribution for breakfast, lunch and dinner. Subsidies can be configured per employee, department or role.

Which devices do we need?

A Face Kiosk or Face Print R5 at the counter. Both read face, fingerprint and card and print meal slips. We confirm the right setup for your counters in the demo.

What reports do we get?

Daily and monthly food counts, sales, popular items, revenue trends and cost analysis.
