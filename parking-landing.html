How it works

One drive through, four things handled

What happens to a vehicle from the moment it reaches your gate until the barrier closes behind it.

  1. Stop 1 · Arrive

    Plate captured

    The entry ANPR camera reads the number plate as the vehicle pulls up.

  2. Stop 2 · Enter

    Checked in

    Check-in time is recorded, a QR token or welcome message appears and the barrier lifts.

  3. Stop 3 · Park

    Stay tracked

    Duration and charges are tracked in real time; overstays raise an alert.

  4. Stop 4 · Leave

    Paid & out

    Exit ANPR finds the vehicle, the fee is shown and the barrier opens once it is paid.

Choose your flow

Three ways to run the same car park

All three use ANPR cameras, boom barriers and real-time dashboards. The difference is how much your staff do at the exit.

Automated Flow

Staff-managed exits with QR tokens and manual payment collection.

  1. Entry detectionThe ANPR camera captures the plate as the vehicle enters.
  2. QR tokenThe plate shows on the kiosk and a QR token prints with its number and check-in time.
  3. Exit processingStaff scan the QR; charges are calculated from the exact stay.
  4. Payment & exitCash or card is collected and the barrier opens on confirmation.

Best suited for

Shopping malls, hospitals and corporate offices that want staff handling exits.

Also includes

VIP & guest waiversOverstay alertsQR token kiosk

Semi-Automated Flow

Automatic detection and app notifications, with staff still collecting payment.

  1. Smart entryANPR reads the plate, records check-in and shows a custom welcome on an Android TV or display.
  2. Exit recognitionThe exit camera detects the departing vehicle.
  3. App popupThe Pro-Parking app shows stay duration and the calculated charge.
  4. Payment & greetingStaff collect payment, the exit display shows a greeting and the barrier opens.

Best suited for

Corporate offices, gated communities and hospitals that want less manual work but keep staff for payment and service.

Also includes

Welcome displayPro-Parking appStaff payment desk

AI Automated Flow

Complete automation with self-service payment kiosks and whitelisting.

  1. Automated entryANPR captures the plate and records check-in with no one at the gate.
  2. Smart trackingThe system monitors parking duration and calculates charges in real time.
  3. Self-service exitThe exit kiosk shows a QR for mobile wallet or card payment.
  4. Instant releaseThe barrier opens as soon as payment is confirmed.

Best suited for

Airports, smart cities and large campuses that need full automation at scale.

Also includes

VIP whitelistingBlacklist restrictionsOperational reports

FAQ

Before you book

Can't find your question? Call us and talk to the team that installs it.

+91 89258 14088
Which flow is right for us?

It depends on how much you want staff involved. The Automated Flow keeps staff at the exit, the Semi-Automated Flow reduces their workload and the AI Automated Flow runs without them. We recommend one after seeing your site in the demo.

Do drivers need to install anything?

No. Plates are read by the ANPR cameras. Depending on the flow, drivers take a QR token, pay at the desk or pay at a self-service kiosk.

Will it work with our cameras and barriers?

Pro-Parking works with ANPR cameras, boom barriers, payment kiosks, mobile apps and existing facility management systems. Our team confirms compatibility with your hardware during the demo.

Can VIPs, residents and guests park free?

Yes. VIP vehicles can be whitelisted, guests can be given fee waivers and blacklisted plates can be restricted at the gate.

How are charges worked out?

From the check-in and check-out times recorded by the cameras, so every fee matches the exact time parked, with no manual counting.

How long does it take to go live?

It depends on your number of gates and the hardware involved. We share a clear rollout plan after understanding your setup.
