Our Story

From the floor to the cloud

We started where the data lives — on factory floors, reception desks and parking gates. Through years of biometric access systems, attendance devices and facility automation, we saw the same pattern everywhere: hardware worked, but the paperwork around it didn't.

IN4 Solutions was built to close that gap. Our software takes the physical signals your organization already generates — attendance punches, visitor visits, vehicle movements — and turns them into clean, accurate, connected digital operations.

  • Business Automation Division

    Part of In4 Solution Pvt Ltd with two decades of on-ground implementation experience.

  • One Connected Ecosystem

    Workforce, visitor and parking operations managed through a single partner.

  • Built Around Physical Operations

    Every product accounts for sites, shifts, security desks and facility realities.
