Our Journey

From the floor to the cloud

We started where the data lives — factory floors, reception desks and parking gates. Hardware worked, but the paperwork around it didn't. IN4 Solutions closes that gap by turning the signals your site already generates — attendance punches, visitor visits, vehicle movements — into clean, connected digital operations.

  • Canteen Management

    Digital meal ordering, token tracking and billing for a smooth employee dining experience.

  • Parking Management

    Vehicle entry, slot allocation and live occupancy across every parking level.

  • AI Video Analytics

    Camera feeds turned into real-time alerts for safety, occupancy and attendance.
