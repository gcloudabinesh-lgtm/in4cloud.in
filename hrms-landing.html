FAQ

Questions HR teams ask us

Does it work with our existing biometric devices?

IN4 CloudHR integrates with biometric attendance devices and manages them from one device setup screen. Our team confirms compatibility with your devices during the demo.

Can we start with attendance and add payroll later?

Yes. IN4 Attend covers attendance and core HR, and you can move up to IN4 People for payroll and employee services when you are ready.

Does payroll handle statutory compliance?

Payroll calculates salary, tax and compliance, and produces payslips and statutory compliance reports as part of every run.

Is there a mobile app for employees?

Yes. The employee self-service app covers check-in, leave, payslips, expense claims, GPS tracking for field staff and notifications.

Can it manage several companies or locations?

Multi-location attendance is built in, and IN4 Enterprise adds multi-company and group company support.

Will it connect to our ERP?

IN4 CloudHR integrates with ERPs such as Tally, SAP and Oracle, as well as custom ERPs. IN4 Enterprise adds API and third-party integrations.
