The Problem

Paper visitor books cost you more than you think

Reception chaos, unknown visitors on-site, and no record of who came, when — or why.

Paper Registers

Unreadable handwriting, missed signatures and zero accountability.

Slow Check-ins

Visitors queue at the front desk while hosts wait for a call.

No Visibility

Nobody knows who is on-site right now — or where they should go.

Weak Audit Trail

Hard to reconstruct visits for security incidents or compliance.

No Host Alerts

Hosts are never notified proactively when their guest arrives.

Unverified Guests

No digital identity check or pre-approval before entry.

The Solution

A complete digital visitor journey

Every interaction — from pre-booking to check-out — captured in one secure system.

Visitor Registration

Pre-register visitors with a QR pass or self-service kiosk check-in.

Host Notification

Hosts are alerted the moment their visitor arrives and checks in.

Digital Check-in

Fast, contactless check-in that validates the visitor in seconds.

Visitor Tracking

Know exactly who is on-site, where they are headed and with whom.

Check-out & Reports

Digital check-out with a complete, exportable visitor audit trail.

Security Management

Role-based controls, badges and a security dashboard at the gate.
